# Business Context

## Client Profile

**Client:** Confidential Regional Food Safety & Regulatory Authority  
**Sector:** Public Health / Regulatory Operations  
**Primary Concern:** Food-business compliance visibility and effective inspection prioritisation

## Why This Engagement Was Commissioned

A recent management review identified several issues:

1. regulatory teams use different extracts and spreadsheets;
2. rating data is not consistently joined to business-type and geographic analysis;
3. inspection recency is not routinely visible;
4. local-authority workload comparisons are inconsistent;
5. component scores are underused in management reporting;
6. leadership wants clearer evidence to support prioritisation of limited regulatory resources.

## Stakeholders

### Director of Regulatory Services — Project Sponsor
Needs an overview of compliance patterns and inspection pressure.

### Head of Environmental Health
Needs establishment-level visibility of lower ratings, old inspections and pending ratings.

### Food Safety Operations Lead
Needs practical operational indicators that can support work planning.

### Public Health Intelligence Lead
Needs careful interpretation of geography, business type and rating patterns.

### Local Authority Performance Lead
Needs comparable measures across local authorities while accounting for differences in portfolio size and scheme.

### Data & Analytics Lead
Needs reproducible ingestion, documented definitions and a maintainable analytical product.

## Decision Context

The organisation is considering how to improve:

- inspection prioritisation;
- performance monitoring;
- local-authority support;
- targeted communications;
- data-quality assurance;
- recurring regulatory reporting.

The analytical product should support professional judgement, not replace statutory decision-making.
